BUILD YOUR LAYOUT

A place for the details
you want to keep visible.

01Screen Regions
Choose a region of your screen and place it where you want in your HUD.
02Image Layers
Add PNG or JPEG images, including transparent PNGs. Reorder layers and adjust each layer’s opacity.
03Precise HUD layout
Move and resize layers precisely. Use a transparent background to keep your layout out of the way.
04Profiles
Save different layouts as profiles. Preview your changes, then use Save & Apply to update your HUD.
05Multi-monitor sources
Capture from one monitor and show your HUD on another. Use Xbox Game Bar to position and pin it.
06Local processing
Your screen captures, profiles and imported images stay on your device.
