Your screen captures

RegionHUD captures the display you choose and shows your selected regions in an Xbox Game Bar overlay. It captures the display before cropping your regions. This happens on your PC.

You choose which display and regions to show, and which images to add.

What stays on your device

Profiles, settings and imported images are stored on your PC. Settings include your source display, layer positions, sizes, order and opacity. Imported images are copied into the app’s local storage for use in your layouts.

Local logs can contain app versions, errors and configuration details. Recovery copies may include earlier settings. Review these files before sharing them.

Resetting or uninstalling RegionHUD can remove local profiles, settings and imported images. Keep anything you need before doing so.

Sharing and accounts

RegionHUD does not send your screen captures, profiles or imported images to WeDidInstead or external servers.

RegionHUD does not include telemetry, analytics, user accounts or cloud synchronization.

This information describes RegionHUD. Windows, Xbox Game Bar and Microsoft Store are separate services.

Your choices and support

To stop using the overlay, close the RegionHUD widget in Xbox Game Bar. Closing the Configurator alone leaves the overlay running.

If you contact support, you choose what to share. Remove private information from screenshots and logs first. RegionHUD does not send support reports automatically.
