How do I open RegionHUD?

Press Win+G, open the Xbox Game Bar widget menu and choose RegionHUD. Pin the widget to keep it visible while using a game or app.

You need Windows 10 (build 19041 or later) or Windows 11 on an x64 PC, with Xbox Game Bar installed and enabled.

Does RegionHUD start automatically?

Yes. RegionHUD starts the components it needs automatically when the widget opens. If it stays on the waiting screen, close the widget and Configurator, wait briefly, then reopen RegionHUD through Game Bar.

How do I configure screen regions?

Open Configure, choose Source Display, then select Add Screen Region and drag a rectangle in the Screen preview. Move and resize the layer in HUD Layout, or enter values in the Inspector.

Press Enter to confirm a typed value. Escape or leaving the field restores unconfirmed text. Use Save & Apply to update the live HUD. Add Image adds a PNG or JPEG, including transparent PNGs.

How do I move the HUD?

Press Win+G, then drag the RegionHUD widget by its title bar. Pin it to keep the HUD visible afterward.

To move a layer inside the HUD, drag it in HUD Layout or change its position in the Inspector.

How do Profiles work?

Use New to create a profile or Duplicate to copy your current layout. The selected profile is the one you are editing; the applied profile is the one shown in the live HUD.

Switching profiles does not change the live HUD. Select a profile and use Save & Apply when you want to use it.

If you have unsaved edits, Save keeps them without applying, Discard drops them, and Cancel stays on the current profile.

What is the difference between Save and Save & Apply?

Save keeps your edits without changing the live HUD. Ctrl+S does the same.

Save & Apply saves the selected profile and updates the live HUD. Until then, the editor preview and live HUD may look different.

How do Source Display and multiple monitors work?

Source Display chooses the monitor to capture. You can show the HUD on a different monitor by moving the widget through Xbox Game Bar.

If the capture looks wrong or a monitor was disconnected, check Source Display. RegionHUD remembers each display’s crop when you switch between them. Use Save & Apply to update the HUD.

How do I zoom or pan while editing?

Use the mouse wheel over Screen or HUD Layout to zoom, or drag with the middle mouse button to pan. Fit shows the whole canvas; 100% returns to its original preview scale. Each preview has its own view controls, which do not change your saved layout.

Why can't I see RegionHUD in Game Bar?

Check that Xbox Game Bar is installed and enabled and that RegionHUD is installed for your Windows user. Reopen Game Bar and check its widget menu. If RegionHUD is still missing, restart Windows. Contact support if the problem continues.

Why is a region blank, missing or showing the wrong area?

Check Source Display, the crop area and the layer’s Visible setting, then use Save & Apply. If the capture stays blank, try your game or app’s windowed or borderless mode, if available. Display-mode compatibility can vary.

For an invisible layer, check its opacity, Global HUD Opacity and layer order. Follow Global HUD Opacity combines the layer’s opacity with the global setting. The background has separate controls. For a missing image, reimport a readable PNG or JPEG.

How do I reset or recover a layout?

For unconfirmed text, press Escape or leave the field to restore the previous value. To drop unsaved changes, choose Discard when closing the editor or switching profiles. This does not undo saved changes.

Before making large edits, keep a separate profile with a working layout. Select it and use Save & Apply to return to it.

If your layout appears empty, check the selected profile and Source Display before saving over it. If needed, close the widget and contact support. Avoid resetting or uninstalling first: this can remove your local profiles, settings and images.

What should I include in a support request?

Include what happened, the steps to reproduce it, and your RegionHUD and Windows versions. For display issues, add your Game Bar version, screen resolution and scaling. Share screenshots or log excerpts only if you choose, after removing private information. Do not send credentials.
